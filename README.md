## Hi there, I'm Seb (the Canadian)!

Sociologist by training, information architect by trade. I work on data governance,
AI enablement, and how organizations actually metabolize technological change —
versus how they perform metabolizing it.

Building things at **[@CognitiveArchitecture](https://github.com/CognitiveArchitecture)** —
a space for reflecting on and reclaiming our relationship with digital tools and data.

## How I think about this

- Integrity before efficiency.
- Friction is signal, not noise.
- Stewardship over capture — tools should be portable and governable.
- Knowledge is a means, not an end.

Touchstones: Illich on conviviality, Ostrom on commons governance,
Aurelius on what deserves your attention.
